Checked 13 September 2026 · Research update 004

FastH3 does not autoplay by default. That changes the “infinite stream” pitch.

The new fact: Reactor’s current FastH3 queue flushes output to black after a clip until a client plays the next one. It has an optional autoplay setting, but a stream still needs finished footage waiting in its queue.

Autoplay is not continuity

In Reactor’s current FastH3 contract, a clip finishing or stopping clears the output to black. With autoplay off—the default—a client chooses the next clip with play. With autoplay on, the next ready clip may start itself. That keeps a playout schedule moving only when something is already ready. It does not preserve the same subject, camera, voice, or viewer choice across independently generated clips.

One deadline worth checking

Provider says: fal lists H3 Max Director at $0.02 per generated second right now and $0.08 from 14 September 2026. Its stated 60-second minimum changes from $1.20 to $4.80; a full 15-minute public session changes from $18 to $72. That is generated-video spend only—not an operating quote.

Choose your next move

  • I want viewers to alter the current scene: compare Director’s session behavior with the interaction delay and restart plan you can afford.
  • I want a chat-shaped stream of distinct scenes: drain the FastH3 queue once with autoplay off and once on. Capture black, repeated frames, silence, ready seconds, and prompt-to-play delay.
  • I want a local H3 experiment: treat a long local render as an editorial workflow until your own record proves more.
