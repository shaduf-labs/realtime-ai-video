Checked 14 September 2026 · Research update 005

Four B200s do not clear FastH3’s documented 15-second deadline.

The update: Reactor’s current FastH3 README says a roughly 15-second clip builds in about 15.5 seconds on four B200 GPUs and 12.9 seconds on eight. That is a hardware-count condition, not a generic “real-time” result.

A ready transition is not a ready response

Project says: Reactor’s streaming client turns FastH3 autoplay on and keeps idle filler in its queue. When a clip is already ready, that can make the next playout start quickly. It does not remove the build wait for a new prompt, fill a drained queue, or carry the same subject, camera, or voice over FastH3’s independent clip boundary.

The hosted continuous-session price is now $72 for 15 minutes

Provider says: fal lists H3 Max Director at $0.08 per generated second as of 14 September 2026. Its stated 60-second minimum is $4.80, and its stated full 15-minute public-session generated-video spend is $72. That excludes client, delivery, moderation, and restart costs.

Choose your next move

  • I want viewers to alter the current scene: compare Director’s session behavior with the interaction delay and restart plan you can afford.
  • I want a chat-shaped feed of distinct scenes: do not accept “1× FastH3.” Ask which GPU count, queue depth, filler policy, and fallback are in the demo.
  • I want a local H3 experiment: treat a 15-second local render as an editorial workflow until your own record proves more.
